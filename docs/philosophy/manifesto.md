# SubWise Manifesto

SubWise is not a budgeting app.

It does not optimise.
It does not persuade.
It does not judge.
It does not try to change its users.

It exists for one purpose:
to make recurring commitments visible.

Home reflects who you are today.
The Journal preserves who you were.
Subscription Detail tells the story of one commitment.
Awareness shows the one truth that deserves your attention today.

SubWise remembers because truth changed.

Nothing more.
Nothing less.
