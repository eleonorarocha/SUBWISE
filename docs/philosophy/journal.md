# The Journal Constitution

The Journal does not remember because time has passed.
It remembers because truth has changed.

## 1. Purpose

The Journal exists to preserve who the person was — not what happened to their subscriptions, but what became true about them over time. It is not a report. It is not a log. It is memory.

## 2. Relationship with Home

Home reflects the present. The Journal preserves the journey. They never compete: Home shows what is true today; the Journal shows what has been true, in sequence.

## 3. What the Journal records

Not events. Truths.

An event is something that happened (a price changed, a renewal occurred). A truth is something that became the case, and remained the case until superseded (a category became the largest, a commitment ended, a threshold was crossed).

## 4. What is a Truth?

- Event ≠ Truth. An event is momentary; a truth is a standing fact.
- Change ≠ Significance. Magnitude does not determine what deserves to be remembered.
- History ≠ Memory. History is everything that happened. Memory is what remains worth carrying forward.

A fact deserves to become a Journal entry when it allows the person to tell a story about themselves that they could not tell before — because something ended, something was revealed, or something was confirmed against expectation.

## 5. Editorial Principles

- One idea at a time.
- Never repeat knowledge.
- Silence is a valid state.
- Facts over opinions.
- Truth over novelty.
- Observations, not statistics.
- A fact should be worth repeating to someone else.
- History of truths, not events.
- Each narrative owns its own memory.
- The Journal never interprets reality.
- Meaning belongs to the reader.
- The Journal is its own baseline: each new truth is compared against the last truth recorded within its own narrative, not against an external average or a fixed starting point.

## 6. The Narratives

There is no single "Journal." There are narratives — independent threads, each with its own memory and its own baseline:

- Recurring Spending
- Streaming
- Subscriptions
- Trials
- Categories

Each evolves on its own timeline. A new entry in one narrative does not require, and should not force, an entry in another.

## 7. What never belongs

- Reminders
- Warnings
- Advice
- Optimisation
- Budgeting
- Coaching
- Statistics
- Dashboards

## 8. The Final Test

If this sentence disappeared forever, would the history of this person become less true?

If the answer is no, it does not belong in the Journal.
